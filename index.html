Frequently asked questions

What is PS Cafe Manager?

A desktop application that helps PlayStation cafe owners manage sessions, bookings, devices, pricing, customers, and revenue from one place.

Who is PS Cafe Manager designed for?

Owners and staff of PlayStation and gaming cafes — from single-room setups to multi-branch operations.

Does it support PS4 and PS5?

Yes — PS4, PS4 Room, PS5, and custom device types are all supported.

Can I create different prices for different devices?

Yes — each device or device type can have its own hourly price, minimum charge, and special pricing.

Can I manage bookings?

Yes — create, change, or cancel reservations, and overlapping bookings are prevented automatically.

Can I track revenue?

Yes — view daily, weekly, and monthly revenue, plus a breakdown by device and device type.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes — the full interface is available in English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout.

Can multiple staff members use the system?

Yes — create staff accounts with Owner, Manager, or Staff roles and set specific permissions for each.

Can I export reports?

Yes — generate daily, weekly, and monthly reports and export them to PDF or Excel/CSV.

Is my data backed up?

Yes — PS Cafe Manager supports automatic backups, manual backups, and full data restore.
